A video studio builtaround attention

We help local showrooms, growing brands and creators look better on screen and get more people to take action.

Built in Gwalior. Driven by a bigger vision.

Mad Monkey Media started in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, eight years ago with a simple belief: every brand has a story worth telling, and every story deserves to be told right.

Founded by Krishna Gupta, what began as a creative vision has grown into an independent video production and creative studio, working with brands across India, the USA and Vietnam.

Over the years, we've worked on countless projects, collaborated with different businesses, and helped brands turn their ideas into stories that connect with people. Every project has taught us something, and every client has been a part of our journey.

Today, we create vertical reels, cinematic commercials and digital content systems for brands that want to stand out and grow.

We're still rooted in Gwalior, but our vision has always been bigger than a location.

Behind every project is a team that cares about the details, takes ownership of the work, and believes in doing things properly.

If you have a story to tell, we'd love to be a part of it.
